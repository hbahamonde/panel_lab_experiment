\IRBTitle{Attachment 6}{Study-Specific Information and Consent}

Participants have already accepted TOKA's general laboratory rules and TOKA privacy notice when joining the PCRC participant system. The following short confirmation concerns this particular study and appears before any practice or paid decision.

\textbf{Study:} \StudyTitle. One approximately 60-minute session with anonymous interaction in five-person groups. Participants remain in a matching pool of 10 or 15 and complete two blocks of ten paid rounds. Payment is EUR~10 plus a decision-based bonus of up to EUR~5.

Participation is voluntary. Foreseeable risks are minimal and consist mainly of waiting in an interactive task and possible mild discomfort with a fictional crisis or unequal point allocations. The interface uses a participant code. PCRC keeps identifying recruitment and payment information separate, gives researchers only coded data, and deletes the temporary identity-to-code link after payment. Further details appear in the participant information sheet and privacy notice.
