\IRBTitle{Attachment 6}{Study-Specific Information and Consent}

Participants receive \irbreplace{the host laboratory's}{UTU Choice Lab's UTU RPP/TOKA} general participation rules and privacy information when registering with its participant system. The following short confirmation concerns this particular study and appears before any practice or paid decision.

\textbf{Study:} \StudyTitle. One approximately 60-minute session with \irbreplace{anonymous interaction}{interaction without on-screen names} in five-person groups. Participants remain in a matching pool of \irbreplace{10 or 15}{5, 10, or 15} and complete \irbreplace{two blocks of ten paid rounds}{ten paid rounds in Block 1 and five in Block 2}. \irbadd{Larger pools are reshuffled each round; a five-person pool plays together throughout.} Payment is EUR~10 plus a decision-based bonus of up to EUR~5.

Participation is voluntary. Foreseeable risks are minimal and consist mainly of waiting in an interactive task and possible mild discomfort with a fictional crisis, unequal point allocations, or optional questions about political views and past party vote. The interface uses a participant code. \irbreplace{The host laboratory keeps identifying recruitment and payment information separate and gives researchers only coded data. The research team never receives any temporary identity-to-code link; the host laboratory removes it after payment under its procedure.}{UTU RPP/TOKA keeps registration and attendance information separately and does not receive oTree codes, experimental responses, or payment information. The research team makes immediate payments using a separate code-and-amount list, which is deleted after payment reconciliation.} Further details appear in the participant information sheet and privacy notice.
