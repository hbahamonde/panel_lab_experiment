\IRBTitle{Attachment 11}{Privacy Notice for Scientific Research}

You are participating in a scientific study at the University of Turku. This notice explains how your personal data are processed in the study \emph{\StudyTitle} under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

\textbf{Responsible researcher and contact for research-related matters:} Janne Tukiainen, Professor, Turku School of Economics, University of Turku; \StudyEmail; \StudyPhone.

\sectionbar{2. Data Protection Officer}
University of Turku Data Protection Officer: \href{mailto:dpo@utu.fi}{dpo@utu.fi}; telephone +358 29 450 3009.

\irbreplace{Each host laboratory separately administers its participant register under its own privacy notice. If a session is held at Tampere DMLab, questions about DMLab's ORSEE register may be directed to the Tampere University Data Protection Officer: \href{mailto:dpo@tuni.fi}{dpo@tuni.fi}.}{UTU Choice Lab separately administers the UTU RPP/TOKA participant register. Its privacy notice is available at }\url{https://toka.utu.fi/tietosuoja/}.

\sectionbar{3. Research and purpose of processing}
The study examines how 300 completed adult participants make consequential group decisions about a public-services fund and whether choices over group approval change after a fictional public-service crisis either improves or persists. Participants remain in matching pools of 10 or 15 and are reshuffled into five-person groups. Personal data are processed to \irbreplace{recruit and compensate participants}{administer immediate participant payments}, run the interactive task, preserve the randomized design, analyse decisions and questionnaire responses, verify results, and report scientific findings. \irbadd{UTU RPP/TOKA administers recruitment and attendance separately from this research dataset.}

\sectionbar{4. Persons processing the data}
Janne Tukiainen, Hector Bahamonde, and authorised personnel may process coded research data. \irbreplace{Personnel at the host laboratory process identifying scheduling, attendance, and payment information and any temporary identity-to-code link.}{UTU Choice Lab personnel process the separate UTU RPP/TOKA registration and attendance records. The research team processes any information needed for immediate payment in a separate payment record that contains no oTree participant code.} University IT personnel may administer the servers without using the data for research. External collaborators receive only anonymised data unless their role and lawful access are documented before disclosure.

\sectionbar{5. Duration of processing}
Personal data are processed from recruitment through data collection, planned for 1--31 October 2026, and subsequent analysis and reporting. \irbreplace{The host laboratory removes any identity-to-code link when compensation has been paid under its procedure. Separate payment and accounting records are retained only as required by administrative and legal obligations. Coded research data without direct identifiers are retained for verification and compatible future scientific research. Their continued necessity is reviewed five years after publication of the primary results and regularly thereafter; data no longer needed for an approved scientific purpose are securely deleted or irreversibly anonymised.}{The separate code-and-amount list used for immediate payment is deleted after payment reconciliation and no later than 30 November 2026. Named payment and accounting records are retained separately for six years after the end of the financial year in which the study is completed, then securely deleted. After the temporary payment list is deleted and the absence of an identity-to-code link is verified, the retained research dataset is anonymised and may be retained for verification and compatible future scientific research.}

\sectionbar{6. Lawful basis}
Processing is based on a task carried out in the public interest for scientific research: GDPR Article 6(1)(e) and section 4(3) of the Finnish Data Protection Act (1050/2018). \irbadd{Where the research team processes a name or other information to make the agreed payment, the basis is GDPR Article 6(1)(b). Any required report to the Finnish Tax Administration is based on GDPR Article 6(1)(c), a legal obligation.} Acceptance of \irbreplace{the host laboratory's}{UTU RPP/TOKA's} general rules and the study-specific ethical consent to participate are documented separately; neither is used as the lawful basis for personal-data processing.

\sectionbar{7. Personal data included}
The research dataset may contain:
\begin{itemize}
  \item participant, session, laboratory-site, matching-pool, pool-size, and temporary-group codes;
  \item the study-specific consent response, form version, and timestamp;
  \item randomized condition, method ballots, uniformly randomized leader selection, proposals, transfers, approval votes, allocations, contributions, group outcomes, and payoffs;
  \item timestamps, response durations, delay flags, practice and comprehension responses;
  \item unpaid expectations and eleven post-task process responses about the experimental situation, approval, equal allocation, and limits on a leader's authority;
  \item twelve optional background responses: age group, gender, education, household financial strain, party voted for in Finland's 2023 parliamentary election, left--right placement, democratic support, satisfaction with democracy in Finland, evaluation of a strong leader unconstrained by parliament and elections, external political efficacy, generalized trust, and risk willingness; and
  \item limited technical log information required for security and troubleshooting.
\end{itemize}
\irbreplace{Names, contact details, and information required for recruitment or payment are held by the host laboratory, are not entered into the experimental interface, and are kept separately from research responses. Researchers receive only coded experimental data.}{Names and contact details in UTU RPP/TOKA are not entered into the experimental interface. Any payment information collected by the research team is kept in a separate payment record that contains no oTree code. The oTree dataset contains coded experimental data only.}

\sectionbar{8. Special categories of personal data}
The final questionnaire directly asks which party the participant voted for in Finland's 2023 parliamentary election, left--right placement, and attitudes toward democracy, political influence, and unconstrained leadership. These answers reveal or may reveal political opinions and are treated as special-category personal data. Their processing is necessary for scientific research in the public interest under GDPR Article 9(2)(j) and section 6(1)(7) of the Finnish Data Protection Act, subject to GDPR Article 89 safeguards. Every background item is optional. The study does not ask about health, ethnicity, religion, sexual orientation, biometric data, or criminal records.

\sectionbar{9. Sources of personal data}
Research responses are obtained directly from participants through oTree during the laboratory session. \irbreplace{The host laboratory obtains scheduling and payment information directly from participants through its participant system and normal administrative processes and assigns the session code. UTU Choice Lab uses UTU RPP/TOKA; DMLab uses ORSEE.}{UTU RPP/TOKA obtains registration and attendance information for the separate participant register. oTree generates the study code. The research team may obtain a name or other information needed for immediate payment, but does not enter it in oTree or UTU RPP/TOKA.} The study does not link experimental responses to public registers or external commercial datasets.

\sectionbar{10. Protection measures and direct identifiers}
The interface collects research data without direct identifiers and uses participant codes. \irbreplace{The host laboratory keeps any temporary identity-to-code link separately and removes it once payment is complete under its procedure; the research team never receives it. The production oTree application and coded data are hosted on University of Turku IT servers with an \texttt{utu.fi} domain in Finland, including for sessions conducted at DMLab.}{The oTree code is not entered into UTU RPP/TOKA. The research team uses a separate temporary code-and-amount list for immediate payment; any named payment receipt does not contain the code. The code-and-amount list is securely deleted after payment reconciliation. The production oTree application and coded data are hosted on University of Turku IT servers with an \texttt{utu.fi} domain in Finland.} Institutional credentials, access control, encrypted connections, managed backups, logging, and two-factor authentication where available protect the data. Results are reported only in aggregate or de-identified form.

\sectionbar{11. Transfers and recipients}
The coded research dataset is not transferred outside the University of Turku as controller. \irbreplace{The host laboratory}{UTU Choice Lab} and University IT process only the information required for their operational roles. \irbdel{If DMLab is used, Tampere University independently controls its separate ORSEE recruitment and payment records under DMLab's privacy notice; those records are not part of the University of Turku research dataset.} The public prototype is not used for live research data.

\sectionbar{12. Transfers outside the EU or EEA}
Personal data are not transferred outside Finland or outside the European Union or European Economic Area.

\sectionbar{13. Processing after the research}
\irbreplace{Coded research data are retained without direct identifiers on University IT-provided storage to assess reliability and for compatible future scientific research in accordance with GDPR Article 5(1)(b) and (e). The need for retention is reviewed five years after publication of the primary results and regularly thereafter. A new privacy notice will be provided for a new use when required; if participants can no longer be identified, the notice will be made available through the University's website.}{The coded research data are retained on University IT-provided storage through immediate payment reconciliation. By 30 November 2026, the temporary payment list is deleted and the Principal Investigator confirms that no identity-to-code link remains. The retained dataset is then anonymised.} Anonymised data, documentation, analysis code, and non-identifying materials may be retained and shared for reproducibility and future scientific use.

\sectionbar{14. Your rights}
Depending on the circumstances and lawful basis, you may have the right to access, correct, or erase your data, restrict or object to processing, and not be subject to consequential automated decision-making. Data portability does not apply to processing based on a public-interest task. Any research-related limitation is assessed case by case and used only when permitted by law and necessary to avoid preventing or seriously impairing the research. Contact Janne Tukiainen to exercise a right.

\irbreplace{Before payment, the host laboratory may be able to locate a record using its separate link. Once the host laboratory removes that link, the research team cannot identify which coded record is yours. GDPR Article 11 then applies unless you provide additional information that reliably identifies the record. Requests concerning the separate participant register are handled under the host laboratory's privacy notice.}{If you want to ask about or exercise a right concerning your experimental record, contact Janne Tukiainen before the temporary payment list is deleted and provide your oTree code. Once that list is deleted, the research team cannot connect a coded record to you; GDPR Article 11 may then apply. Requests concerning the separate UTU RPP/TOKA participant register are handled under the TOKA privacy notice.} The study does not use automated decision making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.
