\textbf{Research project:} \StudyTitle

\textbf{Place and Principal Investigator:} PCRC Decision Laboratory, University of Turku; Janne Tukiainen, Professor, Turku School of Economics (\StudyEmail; \StudyPhone).

You are invited to participate in a one-session study of group decisions and public-service provision. The session lasts approximately 60 minutes and contains two blocks of ten paid rounds in anonymous five-person groups. You will choose whether a selected participant's proposal requires approval, make decisions involving experimental points, and observe the resulting payoffs. Your ten-person matching set is reshuffled between rounds.

You receive a EUR~10 participation payment plus a performance bonus of up to EUR~5. One round from each block is randomly selected, and the points earned in those two rounds determine the bonus. Your total payment is at most EUR~15.

Foreseeable risks are minimal. Some fictional public-service scenarios, unequal allocations, or waiting during an interactive task may be mildly uncomfortable. There is no direct personal benefit beyond payment.

Participation is voluntary. You may refuse or stop without giving a reason and without negative consequences. Because the task is interactive, please inform the laboratory manager if you wish to stop. Data collected before withdrawal may continue to be used for scientific research in the public interest as explained in the privacy notice.

The experimental interface does not require your name. PCRC retains identifying information for recruitment and payment and gives researchers only coded responses. PCRC deletes the identity-to-code link after payment. The coded data may be retained for compatible future scientific research. Results may be published or presented, but individual participants will not be identified.

  \item I understand that participation is voluntary and that I may stop without giving a reason or suffering negative consequences.
  \item I have had an opportunity to ask questions.
  \item I understand that coded research data may be retained for compatible future scientific research as described in the privacy notice.
  \item I voluntarily agree to participate in the research.
\end{itemize}
\end{infobox}
