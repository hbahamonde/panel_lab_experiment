Debriefing

The study examines whether support for concentrating authority changes when ordinary institutions recover from a performance crisis. Some matching pools received an improvement in the productivity of the constrained procedure; others remained in the original low-capacity environment. The executive procedure itself did not change.

The study does not interpret changing one's choice as inconsistency. Updating a decision after receiving new information or experience is the behavioral process the experiment is designed to study.
