Thank you for considering participation in this research study.

The study examines how people make group decisions about public services when conditions change. You will make anonymous decisions with other participants and see the results of those decisions.

Study format: one laboratory session with two decision blocks
Expected duration: approximately 60 minutes

Your decisions are confidential, and other participants will never see your identity.
