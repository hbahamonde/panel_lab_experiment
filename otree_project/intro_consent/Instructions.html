The experiment consists of two synchronized laboratory sessions:

  • Wave 1: {{ wave1_date_display }}
  • Wave 2: {{ wave2_date_display }}

Each wave contains ten paid decision rounds. In every round, five participants vote privately between a constrained collective procedure and an executive-delegation procedure. The procedure receiving at least three votes governs that round and changes how the group's public-service budget is produced.

You will be rematched anonymously within a small matching pool. Screen labels do not identify participants across rounds. This means that every decision should be treated as a new consequential choice rather than as an opportunity to build a personal reputation.

One round from each wave is selected randomly for payment after all ten rounds have been completed. You therefore maximize your expected earnings by treating every round as if it will be selected.

You also receive an information budget that carries from Wave 1 to Wave 2. Opening a report costs points; unopened points remain yours and are converted to money at the end. Information can be useful, but acquiring every report is not possible.

The next pages provide practice examples and comprehension questions. Practice decisions do not affect payment.
