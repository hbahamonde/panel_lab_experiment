The experiment consists of two synchronized laboratory sessions:

  • Session 1: {{ wave1_date_display }}
  • Session 2: {{ wave2_date_display }}

Each session contains ten paid decision rounds. In every round, five citizens vote privately between two options: citizens decide or a leader decides. The option receiving at least three votes is used for that round.

You will play with anonymous participants, and the five-person groups will be reshuffled after every round. Screen labels do not identify people across rounds. Treat every round as a new decision; you cannot build a personal reputation with a named partner.

One round from each session is selected randomly for payment after all ten rounds. Treat every round as if it could determine your earnings.

The society may change between sessions. In Session 2, you will not be told in advance whether public services have recovered. The returns produced during the decision rounds will show how effectively the public-service system is working.

The next pages provide practice examples and comprehension questions. Practice decisions do not affect payment.
