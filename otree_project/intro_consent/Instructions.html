The experiment has one laboratory session with two blocks of ten paid decision rounds. In every round, five citizens vote privately between two options: citizens decide or a leader decides. The option receiving at least three votes is used for that round.

Every citizen begins each round with 20 points. Some points may be placed in a shared public-services fund. Points in this fund produce a return that is divided equally among all five citizens. Points that you do not put in the fund remain yours.

You will play with anonymous participants, and the five-person groups will be reshuffled after every round. Screen labels do not identify people across rounds. Treat every round as a new decision; you cannot build a personal reputation with a named partner.

One round from each block is selected randomly for payment after all twenty rounds. Treat every round as if it could determine your earnings.

The public-services fund rates may change between the two blocks. Before Block 2, the screen will show the rate used under each decision method.

The next pages provide practice examples and comprehension questions. Practice decisions do not affect payment.
