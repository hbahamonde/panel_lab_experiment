{{ block title }}How each round works{{ endblock }} {{ block content }}

The first block has {{ rounds }} paid rounds. In each round, every group member starts with 20 points. Points in the public-services fund create group points that are shared equally among all five members.

First, each group member chooses one of the following methods. The method chosen by at least three of the {{ group_size }} members is used for the round.

Each person chooses

Each group member chooses how many of their own 20 points to put in the fund.

Every fund point creates 1.50 group points. These points are shared equally among all five members.

All fund points remain in the fund.

One person chooses for the group

The software selects one group member as the decision-maker. This person sets the number of points that every member must put in the fund.

Every point left in the fund creates 2.50 group points. These points are shared equally among all five members.

Before the group points are created, the decision-maker may move up to 20 fund points to their own payoff.

You choose the method, not the person. If One person chooses for the group is selected, the software chooses among the members who have served as decision-maker least often. Ties are decided at random.

At the end of the round, everyone sees the group choice, the fund outcome, and their own payoff. The group is then reshuffled for the next round.
