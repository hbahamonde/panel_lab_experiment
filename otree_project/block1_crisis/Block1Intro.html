{{ block title }}How each round works{{ endblock }} {{ block content }}

The first block has {{ rounds }} paid rounds. In each round, every group member starts with 20 points. Points in the public-services fund create group points that are shared equally among all five members.

First, each group member chooses one of the following methods. The method chosen by at least three of the {{ group_size }} members is used for the round. The software then selects one citizen to propose the same allocation for everyone and any transfer to their own payoff.

Group approval required

The other four citizens see the selected person's complete proposal. At least three must approve it.

If approved, every fund point left after any transfer creates 1.50 group points, shared equally.

If rejected, everyone chooses their own allocation at the same 1.50 rate.

Decision takes effect directly

The same kind of proposal is implemented without an approval vote by the other citizens.

Every fund point left after any transfer creates 2.50 group points, shared equally.

The selected person may propose moving up to 20 fund points to their own payoff.

You choose the method, not the person. Under either method, the software selects one of the five citizens at random after the method vote. Everyone has the same one-in-five chance in every round.

At the end of the round, everyone sees the method, proposal, approval result where applicable, fund outcome, and own payoff. The group is then reshuffled.
