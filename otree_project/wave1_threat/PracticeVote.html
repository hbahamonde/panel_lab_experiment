Now suppose you are the leader. Every citizen has 10 points. You choose how many points all five citizens must put in the public-services fund. You may then move up to 10 points from that fund to your personal account. Points left in the fund produce a return that is divided equally among all five citizens.

The leader receives the same shared return as everyone else, plus any points moved to the personal account. Moving points out leaves fewer points to support public services.
