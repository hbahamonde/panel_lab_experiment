Now suppose you are the leader. Every citizen has 10 points. You choose the same number of points that all five citizens must put in the public-services fund. You may then move up to 10 points from that fund to your personal account. Every point left in the fund creates 2.50 points for the group, and the resulting return is divided equally among all five citizens.

Every citizen keeps the points not required for the fund and receives the same shared return. The leader also receives any points moved to the personal account. Moving points out leaves fewer points to support public services.
