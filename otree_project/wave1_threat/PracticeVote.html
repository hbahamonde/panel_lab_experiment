Now suppose you are the executive. Every member has 10 points. You choose an equal contribution for all five members and may transfer as many as 10 collected points to yourself. The remaining public account is multiplied and divided equally.

The executive's payoff equals the same group return received by everyone else, plus the personal transfer. A transfer reduces the amount left to produce public services.
