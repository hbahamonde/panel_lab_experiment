Now suppose you are the leader. Every citizen has 10 points. You choose the contribution required from all five citizens and may keep up to 10 collected points for yourself. The points left in the public-service account produce a public return that is shared equally.

The leader receives the same public return as everyone else, plus any points the leader keeps. Keeping points reduces the amount left to produce public services.
