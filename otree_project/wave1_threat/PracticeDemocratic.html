Suppose every citizen has 10 points. Each citizen chooses how many points to keep and how many to put in the public-services fund. Every point in the fund creates 1.50 points for the group, and the resulting return is divided equally among all five citizens.

Your payoff would be:

10 − points you put in the fund + your equal share of the return.
