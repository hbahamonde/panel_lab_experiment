The following two examples are unpaid and use simplified numbers. Their purpose is to make the payoff rules familiar before any measured decision.

General payoff rule: points you do not put in the public-services fund remain yours. Points left in the fund produce a return that is shared equally among all five citizens. When a leader decides, any points moved to the leader's personal account are added only to that leader's payoff.

After the examples, three short questions check that the main rules are clear. You may revise an incorrect answer.
