{{ block title }}How each round works{{ endblock }} {{ block content }}

The first block has {{ rounds }} paid rounds. In each round, every citizen starts with 20 points. Points in the public-services fund create group points that are shared equally among all five citizens.

First, each citizen chooses one of the following decision methods. The method chosen by at least three of the {{ group_size }} citizens is used for the round.

Citizens decide

Each citizen chooses how many of their own 20 points to put in the fund.

Every fund point creates 1.50 group points. These points are shared equally among all five citizens.

No one can take points out of the fund.

A leader decides

The software chooses one citizen as leader. The leader sets the number of points that every citizen must put in the fund.

Every point left in the fund creates 2.50 group points. These points are shared equally among all five citizens.

Before the group points are created, the leader may move up to 20 fund points to their own payoff.

You choose the method, not the leader. If A leader decides is chosen, the software selects a leader from the citizens who have served least often. Ties are decided at random.

At the end of the round, everyone sees the group choice, the fund outcome, and their own payoff. The group is then reshuffled for the next round.
