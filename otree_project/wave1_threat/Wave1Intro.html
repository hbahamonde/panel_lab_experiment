Imagine that you are a citizen in a society where public services have been under serious strain for a long time. Parliament has failed to pass a budget for eight months, healthcare waiting times have doubled, and planning for public services has stalled.

You will complete {{ rounds }} paid rounds. In every round, an anonymous group of {{ group_size }} citizens chooses between two ways of making the public-service decision:

Citizens decide

Each citizen chooses their own contribution. Public returns are shared equally. No citizen can take points from the public-service account.

A leader decides

One citizen is chosen as leader for the round. The leader requires the same contribution from everyone and produces a larger public return, but may keep some collected points.

You also receive {{ info_budget }} points that you may spend on optional reports. These points carry into Session 2. Every information point you do not spend is paid to you at the end.
