You are entering a hypothetical society experiencing a prolonged governance-capacity crisis. Parliament has failed to pass a budget for eight months, healthcare waiting times have doubled, and public-service planning has stalled.

You will complete {{ rounds }} paid rounds. In every round, an anonymous group of {{ group_size }} participants chooses between two procedures:

Constrained collective procedure

Every member controls their own contribution. All public returns are divided equally. No member can take money from the public account.

Executive-delegation procedure

One member can impose an equal contribution and implement the project more efficiently, but may transfer collected points to themself.

You begin with an information budget of {{ info_budget }} points, which carries into Wave 2. Information points you do not spend are paid at the end.
